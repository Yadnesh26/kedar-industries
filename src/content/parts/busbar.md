overview: |
  Busbars with integral pin terminals interconnect the DC side of capacitor banks in power converter and drive assemblies. Mounting-hole position, bend angle and pin location determine how well the busbar seats, how evenly current is shared and how reliably each connection is made.

  Kedar manufactures these busbars in-house to customer drawings: copper is cut, mounting holes are punched and flanges are bent on busbar processing equipment, holding hole position and bend angle consistently across a production run. Every batch is dimensionally inspected against the approved drawing, including export-grade capacitor orders such as Vishay’s WECS (Wind Energy Conversion System) busbars, 200 sets.
