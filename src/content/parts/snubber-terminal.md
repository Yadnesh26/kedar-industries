  Snubber terminals connect snubber capacitors directly across power semiconductors such as IGBTs and thyristors, where they absorb switching transients and limit dv/dt and voltage spikes. Because a snubber is only as effective as its connection, the terminal must keep the current path short and low-inductance, with consistent contact quality.

  Kedar manufactures snubber terminals in volume, blanking, piercing and forming them on power presses for repeatable geometry from part to part. Hole positions, form angles and contact faces are verified through first-piece approval and in-process dimensional checks against the approved drawing, with inspection records kept for every batch.
