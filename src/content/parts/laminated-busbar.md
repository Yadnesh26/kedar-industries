overview: |
  Laminated busbars stack flat copper conductor layers separated by thin insulation, so the opposing currents in adjacent layers cancel each other’s magnetic field. The result is very low stray inductance (ESL), lower voltage overshoot during semiconductor switching and more uniform current distribution than discrete cables or busbars, in a compact, rigid footprint.

  Kedar manufactures laminated busbars to customer drawings, with conductor layers cut, punched and formed for accurate terminal positions and precise layer alignment. Insulation spacing is maintained between layers and at the edges for the required creepage and clearance, and every assembly is dimensionally and visually inspected against the approved drawing before dispatch.
