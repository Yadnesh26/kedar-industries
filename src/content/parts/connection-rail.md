overview: |
  Connection rails are rigid current-carrying conductors that interconnect capacitor terminals and elements within a capacitor assembly, transferring load current from the bar to threaded stud terminations for secure bolted connections. The bar cross-section is sized for the rated current, and stud pitch, stud height and stud angle are held to the customer drawing so every rail fits the assembly without rework.

  Each rail is manufactured in-house: the bar profile is cut and formed with raised stud bosses, threaded studs are fitted square and true to the boss, and edges are finished burr-free. Every batch undergoes first-piece approval and in-process dimensional checks of stud pitch, height and perpendicularity against the approved drawing, with inspection records kept for traceability.
