overview: |
  GLI terminals are lead-in terminations that carry current from the capacitor element’s internal windings to the external circuit. The terminal must combine low contact resistance with joint integrity that holds up under thermal cycling and mechanical load over the capacitor’s service life.

  Kedar manufactures GLI terminals in-house and makes the critical joints by precision soldering and silver brazing, with controlled heat input for complete wetting and a sound joint fillet. Joints and terminal dimensions are checked against the approved drawing through visual and dimensional inspection on every batch, with inspection records maintained for traceability.
