  PEC tubular components are tubular conductors used as terminations and internal connections in capacitor assemblies, where they combine current-carrying capacity with the rigidity of a tubular section. Accurate length, bend geometry and end form are essential so each part locates correctly and terminates reliably in the assembly.

  Kedar manufactures PEC tubulars in volume to the dimensional and material specifications of the capacitor manufacturer: tube stock is cut to length and formed to drawing without cracking or kinking the wall, and ends are finished for clean termination. Dimensions and bend angles are verified through first-piece approval and in-process inspection, with records maintained for traceability across production batches.
