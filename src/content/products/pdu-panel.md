A Power Distribution Unit (PDU) plant with capacity up to 600 kVA is designed to manage and
distribute high electrical loads efficiently across industrial and data centre infrastructure.
It integrates transformers, breakers and intelligent monitoring systems to ensure safe,
balanced and uninterrupted power delivery.

Kedar Industries builds PDU panels in-house. Each outgoing circuit is switched and protected
separately, so a fault on one load does not affect the others.
