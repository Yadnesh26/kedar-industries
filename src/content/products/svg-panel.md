overview: |
  A Static VAR Generator (SVG) panel corrects power factor with an IGBT-based inverter instead
  of capacitor banks. The inverter injects exactly the leading or lagging reactive current the
  load needs, so correction is step-less and holds the power factor close to unity at every
  load level, with no over- or under-compensation. Because there are no capacitors to switch,
  the panel cannot resonate with network harmonics and has no contactors to wear out. It can
  also balance load current drawn by single-phase and two-phase loads. Kedar Industries has
  supplied SVG panels for Sudarshan Chemical Industries, NSK Industries and a residential
  project in Mumbai.

faqs:
  - q: "What is a Static VAR Generator?"
    a: "It is an inverter-based device that generates the reactive power a load needs electronically, instead of switching capacitor banks in and out."
  - q: "How is an SVG different from an APFC panel?"
    a: "An APFC panel switches fixed capacitor steps, so correction comes in steps and takes seconds. An SVG adjusts its output continuously, corrects within about 20 milliseconds, and works for both leading and lagging loads."
  - q: "Will an SVG cause harmonic resonance?"
    a: "No. It contains no power factor correction capacitors, so there is nothing for network harmonics to resonate with."
  - q: "Can it correct load unbalance?"
